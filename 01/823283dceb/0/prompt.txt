## Goal

Two capabilities:
1. A `primary_facility` patient extension — an autocomplete backed by the facility list, shown during patient create and update.
2. A "People" entry in the facility left-nav, at `/facility/:facilityId/people`, listing that facility's patients with identifier-based search.

**Backend is out of scope** — owned by someone else. Assume this contract:
- `GET /api/v1/facility/{facilityId}/people/` → `{ count, results: PatientRead[] }`, accepts `limit` / `offset`.
- `POST /api/v1/facility/{facilityId}/people/search/` → mirrors `/api/v1/patient/search/`: body `{ config, value, page_size }`, response `{ partial: boolean, results }`.
