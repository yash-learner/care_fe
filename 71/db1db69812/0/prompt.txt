VIDEO EMBEDS IN care_docs FLOW DOCS — use relative paths, never absolute URLs.

Place the embed directly under the `## Overview` heading of the flow doc:

<video src="../media/<file>.mp4" poster="../media/<file>.png" controls width="720"></video>

[Watch the demo](../media/<file>.mp4) — <short phrase describing the clip>.

Rules:
- The path is relative to the flow doc: `../media/...`. It is confirmed to render
  on github.com. Do NOT use an absolute https://github.com/<org>/<repo>/raw/... URL.
- <file> is the flow doc's own filename without the extension, e.g.
  `1. Start a discussion`. Video, poster, spec, and doc all share that stem.
- Filenames contain spaces. Encode every space as %20 in the src, the poster,
  and the markdown link.
- The `poster` attribute supplies the thumbnail. Do NOT add a
  [![Watch the demo](...png)](...mp4) fallback line — it is redundant.
- Keep `controls width="720"`.

Reference implementation: care_docs/Clinical/Encounter/flows/create_an_encounter.md
