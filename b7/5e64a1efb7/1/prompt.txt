## Verified facts

This flow describes how to review the symptoms and the diagnoses of a patient, both for the current encounter and across all past encounters.

**Prerequisites**
- You have the "Can view clinical data about patients" permission or the "Can Read encounter related clinical data" permission. Without one of them, the encounter shows "You do not have permission to view clinical data for this encounter".
- You do not need write access. This flow is read-only, and it works for an encounter of any status.

**Entry point A — the current encounter**
1. Open the encounter and go to the **Overview** tab.
2. The **Symptoms** and **Diagnoses** sections show the records of that encounter.
3. When nothing is recorded, the sections show "No Symptoms" with "No symptoms have been recorded", or "No Diagnoses" with "No Diagnoses have been recorded".

**Entry point B — the full patient history**
1. Select **See Clinical History** on the encounter, or press the keyboard shortcut **`h`**.
2. The patient's clinical history page opens. Its title reads "{patient name}'s clinical history".
3. Select the **Past Symptoms** tab or the **Past Diagnoses** tab.
4. The records show grouped by year and then by date, newest first.
5. Each record shows the clinical term, badges for Status, Verification, and Severity, the onset date, and who reported it.
6. Select the note control to open or hide the note text.
7. If the record comes from another encounter, select **Go to Encounter** to open that encounter.
8. Select **Back to Encounter** or **Back to Patient** to leave the history page.

**Reading the badges**
- Status values: Active, Recurrence, Relapse, Inactive, Remission, Resolved.
- Verification values: Unconfirmed, Provisional, Differential, Confirmed, Refuted, Entered in Error.
- Severity values: Mild, Moderate, Severe.
- A record with the Verification value "Entered in Error" was retracted. Do not use it for clinical decisions.

**Permissions section content**
- To view: "Can view clinical data about patients" or "Can Read encounter related clinical data".
- By default, doctors, nurses, administrators, and facility administrators can read encounter clinical data. Staff can view patient clinical data.

## Scope
The module is **Condition**. In CARE, the FHIR R5 `Condition` concept (https://build.fhir.org/condition.html — link it on first mention) is recorded as two related record types: **Symptom** and **Diagnosis**. Document both as parts of one Condition module. Allergy Intolerance is a separate module — do not cover it.

**DO NOT mention "Chronic Condition" anywhere.** That capability is not available yet. Diagnoses recorded in CARE today are always visit-specific and show the category badge "Diagnosis".

## Verified facts (use only these)

**What a condition is in CARE**
- A symptom is what the patient reports or the clinician observes. A diagnosis is the clinician's determination of the condition.
- Every symptom and every diagnosis belongs to one patient and is recorded against one encounter. CARE takes the patient from the encounter; you never choose the patient separately.
- You cannot record a symptom or a diagnosis without an active encounter. The screen then shows "Symptoms cannot be recorded without an active encounter" or "Diagnosis cannot be recorded without an active encounter".

**Attributes (same for symptoms and diagnoses unless stated)**
| Attribute | Description | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Code | The clinical term, selected from a standard SNOMED CT clinical-finding terminology | none, you must select one |
| Status | The current clinical state | Active |
| Verification | How certain the record is | Confirmed |
| Severity | How severe the condition is | Moderate. For a diagnosis you can leave it empty; the field then shows "Choose severity" |
| Onset Date | When the condition started | Today. A future date is not accepted |
| Note | Free text | empty |

Diagnoses additionally show a category badge with the label "Diagnosis", which marks the record as specific to that visit.

**Status values (exact labels):** Active, Recurrence, Relapse, Inactive, Remission, Resolved.
**Verification values (exact labels):** Unconfirmed, Provisional, Differential, Confirmed, Refuted, Entered in Error.
**Severity values (exact labels):** Mild, Moderate, Severe.

**Lifecycle**
- You record symptoms and diagnoses from the encounter's Overview tab, in the "Symptoms" and "Diagnoses" sections.
- You can change the Status, Verification, Severity, and Note of a saved record at any time while the encounter is open. You cannot change the Onset Date after the record is saved.
- CARE never deletes a saved symptom or diagnosis. To retract one, you set its Verification to "Entered in Error". The record stays visible with that label.
- Records marked "Entered in Error" are left out of the past-records picker.
- Symptoms and diagnoses stay with the patient. The patient's clinical history shows them across all encounters, under the "Past Symptoms" and "Past Diagnoses" tabs.

**Terminology**
- Codes come from a standard SNOMED CT clinical-finding terminology. The same terminology serves symptoms and diagnoses. Your deployment's administrator can change the available terminology.

**Who can do what**
- To see symptoms and diagnoses you need "Can view clinical data about patients" or "Can Read encounter related clinical data".
- To record or change them you need "Update Encounter related clinical data".
- By default, doctors, nurses, administrators, and facility administrators can record and change clinical data. Staff can view patient clinical data but do not have encounter clinical-data access.
- All add and edit controls become read-only when the encounter status is Completed, Cancelled, Discontinued, or Entered in Error.

**FHIR R5 alignment (describe in user terms, no field names from code)**
CARE follows FHIR R5 Condition: the code, clinical status, verification status, severity, category, onset, and note all map to the FHIR Condition resource. CARE records the clinical status, verification status, and severity as fixed lists rather than free coded values, and records one note rather than many.

## Verified facts

**Prerequisites**
- The patient has an open encounter at the facility, and that encounter is the patient's active encounter.
- You have the "Update Encounter related clinical data" permission. Doctors, nurses, administrators, and facility administrators have it by default.
- The encounter is not Completed, Cancelled, Discontinued, or Entered in Error. In those states all add and edit controls are read-only.
- Without an active encounter the screen shows "Diagnosis cannot be recorded without an active encounter".

**Entry points**
1. Open the encounter, go to the **Overview** tab, find the **Diagnoses** section, and select the edit (pencil) icon.
2. Press the keyboard shortcut **`d`** anywhere on the encounter screens. This is the fastest path and it opens the same diagnosis screen. (There is no visible quick-action tile for this; the shortcut is the intended alternative.)

**Steps**
1. The diagnosis screen opens with the list of diagnoses already recorded for the encounter.
2. Select **Add Diagnosis**. On a later addition the control reads **Add another Diagnosis**.
3. A search box opens. Type at least **3 characters**. Below that, the screen shows a message that tells you the minimum length.
4. Results come from a standard SNOMED CT clinical-finding terminology. The search also offers a **Recents** tab with codes you used before, and a **Starred** tab with codes you pinned. Select the star icon on a result to pin it.
5. Select a term. A new row appears with these defaults:
   - Status: **Active**
   - Verification: **Confirmed**
   - Severity: **Moderate**
   - Onset Date: **today**
   The row also shows a badge with the label **Diagnosis**, which marks the record as specific to this visit.
6. Change the values if you need to:
   - **Status** (placeholder "Select diagnosis status"): Active, Recurrence, Relapse, Inactive, Remission, Resolved.
   - **Verification** (placeholder "Select verification status"): Unconfirmed, Provisional, Differential, Confirmed, Refuted. ("Entered in Error" is not offered for a new diagnosis.)
   - **Severity**: Mild, Moderate, Severe. You can also leave the severity empty; the field then shows "Choose severity".
   - **Onset Date**: pick a date. A future date is not accepted.
7. To add a note, use the row's **Notes** field, available from the row's more-options (**⋮**) menu.
8. Repeat from step 2 for each further diagnosis. If you select a term that the encounter already has, CARE shows the warning "Diagnosis already exists!" and does not add a second row.
9. Select **Submit**.

**Result**
- CARE shows the confirmation "Questionnaire submitted successfully".
- The diagnoses appear in the **Diagnoses** section of the encounter Overview tab, and in the patient's **Past Diagnoses** clinical history.
- When no diagnosis is recorded, the section shows "No Diagnoses" with the message "No Diagnoses have been recorded".

**Permissions section content**
- To view: "Can view clinical data about patients" or "Can Read encounter related clinical data".
- To record: "Update Encounter related clinical data".
- Without view access the encounter shows "You do not have permission to view clinical data for this encounter".

**Note about configuration**
The available clinical terminology is set for your deployment and your administrator can change it.

This flow describes how to retract a symptom or a diagnosis that you recorded by mistake. CARE never deletes a saved clinical record. It keeps the record and marks it as **Entered in Error**, so the clinical history stays complete and auditable.

**Prerequisites**
- The encounter is the patient's active encounter and its status is not Completed, Cancelled, Discontinued, or Entered in Error.

- Encounter **Overview** tab → **Symptoms** section edit (pencil) icon, or the keyboard shortcut **`s`**.

**Steps**
1. Open the symptom screen or the diagnosis screen and find the row you want to retract.
2. Open the row's more-options (**⋮**) menu.
3. Select the remove action. For a symptom the action reads **Remove Symptom**.
4. What happens next depends on whether the record is saved:
   - **The record is not yet submitted.** CARE removes the row from the list completely. Nothing is kept.
   - **The record is already saved.** CARE sets its Verification to **Entered in Error**. The row stays in the list with that label. The remove action is then unavailable, and its tooltip reads **Already marked as error**.
5. Select **Submit**.

**Result**
- CARE shows the confirmation "Questionnaire submitted successfully".
- The record keeps the Verification value **Entered in Error**.
- Records marked "Entered in Error" are left out of the past-records picker, so you do not carry them into a later encounter.
- To record the correct information, add a new symptom or diagnosis.

**Notes**
- The "Entered in Error" option does not show in the Verification list of a normal record. You set it only with the remove action.

- To view: "Can view clinical data about patients" or "Can Read encounter related clinical data".
- To retract: "Update Encounter related clinical data".

## Scope
The module is **Condition**. In CARE, the FHIR R5 `Condition` concept (https://build.fhir.org/condition.html — link it on first mention) is recorded as two related record types: **Symptom** and **Diagnosis**. Document both as parts of one Condition module. Allergy Intolerance is a separate module — do not cover it.

**DO NOT mention "Chronic Condition" anywhere.** That capability is not available yet. Diagnoses recorded in CARE today are always visit-specific and show the category badge "Diagnosis".

## Verified facts (use only these)

**What a condition is in CARE**
- A symptom is what the patient reports or the clinician observes. A diagnosis is the clinician's determination of the condition.
- Every symptom and every diagnosis belongs to one patient and is recorded against one encounter. CARE takes the patient from the encounter; you never choose the patient separately.
- You cannot record a symptom or a diagnosis without an active encounter. The screen then shows "Symptoms cannot be recorded without an active encounter" or "Diagnosis cannot be recorded without an active encounter".

**Attributes (same for symptoms and diagnoses unless stated)**
| Attribute | Description | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Code | The clinical term, selected from a standard SNOMED CT clinical-finding terminology | none, you must select one |
| Status | The current clinical state | Active |
| Verification | How certain the record is | Confirmed |
| Severity | How severe the condition is | Moderate. For a diagnosis you can leave it empty; the field then shows "Choose severity" |
| Onset Date | When the condition started | Today. A future date is not accepted |
| Note | Free text | empty |

Diagnoses additionally show a category badge with the label "Diagnosis", which marks the record as specific to that visit.

**Status values (exact labels):** Active, Recurrence, Relapse, Inactive, Remission, Resolved.
**Verification values (exact labels):** Unconfirmed, Provisional, Differential, Confirmed, Refuted, Entered in Error.
**Severity values (exact labels):** Mild, Moderate, Severe.

**Lifecycle**
- You record symptoms and diagnoses from the encounter's Overview tab, in the "Symptoms" and "Diagnoses" sections.
- You can change the Status, Verification, Severity, and Note of a saved record at any time while the encounter is open. You cannot change the Onset Date after the record is saved.
- CARE never deletes a saved symptom or diagnosis. To retract one, you set its Verification to "Entered in Error". The record stays visible with that label.
- Records marked "Entered in Error" are left out of the past-records picker.
- Symptoms and diagnoses stay with the patient. The patient's clinical history shows them across all encounters, under the "Past Symptoms" and "Past Diagnoses" tabs.

**Terminology**
- Codes come from a standard SNOMED CT clinical-finding terminology. The same terminology serves symptoms and diagnoses. Your deployment's administrator can change the available terminology.

**Who can do what**
- To see symptoms and diagnoses you need "Can view clinical data about patients" or "Can Read encounter related clinical data".
- To record or change them you need "Update Encounter related clinical data".
- By default, doctors, nurses, administrators, and facility administrators can record and change clinical data. Staff can view patient clinical data but do not have encounter clinical-data access.
- All add and edit controls become read-only when the encounter status is Completed, Cancelled, Discontinued, or Entered in Error.

**FHIR R5 alignment (describe in user terms, no field names from code)**
CARE follows FHIR R5 Condition: the code, clinical status, verification status, severity, category, onset, and note all map to the FHIR Condition resource. CARE records the clinical status, verification status, and severity as fixed lists rather than free coded values, and records one note rather than many.

## Verified facts

This flow describes how to change a symptom or a diagnosis that is already recorded, for example when the patient improves or when a provisional finding becomes confirmed.

**Prerequisites**
- The symptom or the diagnosis is already recorded on the encounter.
- The encounter is the patient's active encounter and its status is not Completed, Cancelled, Discontinued, or Entered in Error. In those states the record is read-only.
- You have the "Update Encounter related clinical data" permission.

**Entry points**
- Encounter **Overview** tab → **Symptoms** section edit (pencil) icon, or the keyboard shortcut **`s`**.
- Encounter **Overview** tab → **Diagnoses** section edit (pencil) icon, or the keyboard shortcut **`d`**.

**Steps**
1. Open the symptom screen or the diagnosis screen. The records of the encounter show in a list, each in its own row.
2. Change the values you need. You can change these fields on a saved record:
   - **Status**: Active, Recurrence, Relapse, Inactive, Remission, Resolved.
   - **Verification**: Unconfirmed, Provisional, Differential, Confirmed, Refuted. The option "Entered in Error" shows only if the record already has that value. To retract a record, see the related flow.
   - **Severity**: Mild, Moderate, Severe. For a diagnosis you can also leave it empty; the field then shows "Choose severity".
   - **Note**: open the row's more-options (**⋮**) menu and select **Add notes**. Use **Show notes** and **Hide notes** to open or close the note text.
3. You cannot change the **Onset Date** of a saved record. The field is unavailable after the first submission.
4. You cannot change the clinical term of a saved record. To correct a wrong term, mark the record as entered in error and record the correct one.
5. Select **Submit**.

**Result**
- CARE shows the confirmation "Questionnaire submitted successfully".
- The updated values show in the **Symptoms** or **Diagnoses** section of the encounter Overview tab, and in the patient's clinical history.

**Permissions section content**
- To view: "Can view clinical data about patients" or "Can Read encounter related clinical data".
- To change: "Update Encounter related clinical data".
